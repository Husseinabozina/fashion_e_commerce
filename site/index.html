03 / THE IDENTITY

A fold.
A direction.
A signature.

Three shapes form one distinctive N. A diagonal seam suggests folded fabric and movement, carrying the streetwear character from the launcher icon into the app.

The native splash and Flutter launch share the same mark. A finite 1.6-second reveal introduces the wordmark; reduced-motion preferences show the final identity immediately.
